Coastal geotechnics
Stability of bluffs, foundations, bridge piers, and road and rail embankments under storms, scour, and erosion.
Geohazards
Storm surge, extreme rainfall, groundwater rise, and freeze–thaw, from scour to slope failure.

Geosystems

Sustainable infrastructure
